About Our Project

Pancreatic cancer is one of the most challenging diseases to diagnose and treat, often because symptoms appear late, and conventional testing methods can be invasive and expensive. Our project addresses these challenges by utilizing cutting-edge, non-invasive methods to predict pancreatic cancer accurately and effectively.

By analyzing data from simple urine and blood tests, we provide a solution that is both accessible and cost-effective. This approach eliminates the need for complex procedures while maintaining high levels of accuracy, making early detection easier and more achievable for patients and healthcare professionals alike.

Our tool is designed to improve public health by enabling early interventions and reducing the burden of late-stage pancreatic cancer cases. With a focus on simplicity and precision, we aim to make this tool accessible to people from all walks of life.
