Health Recommendations

Do's

  • Eat soft and easy-to-digest foods like soups, fruits, and steamed vegetables.
  • Have small meals throughout the day instead of eating a lot at once.
  • Drink water slowly and stay hydrated.
  • Take your medicines on time, exactly as the doctor says.
  • Do light exercises like walking or gentle stretches to stay active.
  • Rest when you feel tired and get enough sleep every day.
  • Visit your doctor regularly and tell them if you notice any changes in your health.
  • Spend time with your family and friends to feel supported.
  • Keep yourself clean and wash your hands often to avoid getting sick.
  • Maintain a positive mindset and engage in stress-relieving activities.

Don'ts

  • Avoid eating oily, fried, or spicy foods as they can upset your stomach.
  • Don’t skip meals or eat too much in one go.
  • Stay away from alcohol and sugary drinks.
  • Don’t stop or skip medicines without asking your doctor.
  • Don’t delay medical checkups.
  • Don’t forget to stay hydrated. Dehydration can worsen symptoms.
  • Don’t engage in heavy physical activities that may strain your body.
  • Avoid stress and lack of sleep as they weaken the immune system.
  • Stay away from tobacco and smoking, as they increase health risks.
  • Don’t ignore any sudden changes in your body—always consult a doctor.
