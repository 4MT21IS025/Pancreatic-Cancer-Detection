Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is this website for?

This website helps in detecting the likelihood of pancreatic cancer using machine learning.

Who can use this system?

Anyone, including patients, doctors, and researchers, can use this tool for quick assessments.

Is the prediction 100% accurate?

No, the model provides a probability based on medical data, but a doctor's diagnosis is necessary.

Is my data stored?

No, we do not store or share any user data entered in the prediction system.

Health & Medical Questions

What are the symptoms of pancreatic cancer?

Common symptoms include jaundice, weight loss, abdominal pain, and digestive issues.

What are the major risk factors?

Smoking, obesity, diabetes, chronic pancreatitis, and genetic factors increase the risk.

Can pancreatic cancer be detected early?

Early detection is difficult, but regular screenings for high-risk individuals can help.

How can I reduce my risk?

Avoid smoking, maintain a healthy weight, eat a balanced diet, and exercise regularly.

Technical Questions

How does the prediction system work?

The system uses a Random Forest model trained on medical datasets to estimate cancer risk.

What input data is required?

Medical parameters such as age, Plasma level, creatinine and other risk factors.

What is the accuracy of this model?

The model provides precision, recall, and accuracy scores, visible after prediction.

Do I need to install anything?

No, the system runs entirely in a web browser.

Can I access this system from my phone?

Yes, the website is fully mobile-friendly.

How is the model updated?

We periodically update the model based on new medical research and improved datasets.
